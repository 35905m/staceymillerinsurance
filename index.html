About Stacey

I'm Stacey Miller, and I work with the Nick Martinez Insurance Agency in Fort Worth.

I've been blind for 15 years. I spent a long time working my way back to living on my own and getting back to work, and I did it.

Losing my sight taught me to really listen. I hear the pause before someone answers, the worry in their voice, the question they almost didn't ask. That's how I help families find coverage that actually fits their life.

I also lead Love of Christ Ministries, a ministry close to my heart.
